Step by step

What to do after a boating accident

Safety first. Then protect your claim.

  1. Get everyone safe and call 911.Get injured people out of the water and get medical help right away.
  2. Report the accident.Make sure the accident is reported to the authorities, such as a conservation police officer or the local sheriff. Confirm reporting rules
  3. Get names and boat information.Operator and owner names, phone numbers, boat registration numbers and insurance information.
  4. Find witnesses.Other boaters and people on shore often see what happened. Get their names and numbers.
  5. Take photos.Both boats, the damage, the location, life jackets and your injuries.
  6. Don't give a recorded statement.Talk to a lawyer before speaking with the other side's insurance company.

Know your rights

Illinois laws that affect boating accident cases

2 years

To file an injury claim

Most boating injury claims must be filed within 2 years of the accident. Claims against a town, county or other local government have a shorter deadline.

Source735 ILCS 5/13-202Illinois Code of Civil Procedure ↗
50% rule

Shared fault

You can still recover as long as you were 50% or less at fault. Your compensation is reduced by your share of the fault.

Source735 ILCS 5/2-1116Illinois Code of Civil Procedure ↗
2 years

Fatal boating accidents

When a boating accident takes a life, the family's wrongful death claim usually must be filed within 2 years of the death.

Source740 ILCS 180/2Illinois Wrongful Death Act ↗

Where the money comes from

Who pays after a boating accident

Auto insurance usually doesn't cover a boat, so we look for the policies that do.

  • The operator's or owner's boat insurance. Many boat owners carry a separate watercraft policy.
  • Homeowner's or umbrella coverage. Some policies cover small boats or add liability coverage on top. Trent/Michael to confirm
  • Rental businesses. Marinas and rental companies that rent boats or personal watercraft may carry their own coverage.
  • Equipment makers. If a defective boat part, engine or life jacket played a role, the manufacturer may be responsible.
  • Your health insurance. It can cover treatment now, and we sort out repayment when the case settles.
Straight answers

Boating accident FAQ

Who is responsible for a boating accident?

Usually the person operating the boat carelessly. The boat's owner, a rental business or the maker of defective equipment may also be responsible.

Does the boat owner's insurance cover my injuries?

If the owner has a watercraft policy, it may. Some homeowner's and umbrella policies also provide coverage. We track down every policy that applies.

I was a passenger. Can I make a claim against the driver of my own boat?

Yes. Passengers can make claims against the operator who caused the accident, even if that's a friend or family member. The claim is usually paid by insurance, not by them personally.

What if the operator was drinking?

Operating a boat while impaired is illegal in Illinois and is strong evidence of carelessness. Tell us if you believe alcohol was involved. Confirm BUI wording

How long do I have to file?

Most Illinois injury claims must be filed within 2 years. Some boating cases can have different rules, so call as soon as you can.

How much does it cost to hire you?

Nothing up front. We work on a contingency fee and are only paid if we recover money for you. If an insurer makes an offer before you hire us, we don't take a fee from that money.
