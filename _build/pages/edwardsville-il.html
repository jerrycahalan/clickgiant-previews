Know your rights

Rules that apply to every Edwardsville injury claim

2 years

To file an injury claim

Most injury claims must be filed within 2 years. Claims against a city, county or other local government have a shorter, 1-year deadline.

Straight answers

Edwardsville injury FAQ

Your offices are in Montgomery County. Can you handle a Madison County case?

Yes. Illinois lawyers can handle cases in any county in the state, and Madison County is where Trent served as a prosecutor. We'll appear at the courthouse in Edwardsville whenever your case needs it.

Do I have to drive to Litchfield?

No. We'll come to your home or hospital room, or meet by phone or video. If you'd rather come in, our Litchfield office is the closest.

I'm an SIUE student and got hurt. Can you help?

Yes. Students have the same rights as anyone else after a crash or fall. If your parents' insurance may apply, we'll sort that out too. Trent/Michael to confirm

Where would my lawsuit be filed?

Most cases settle without a lawsuit. If one is needed, an Edwardsville case will often be filed at the Madison County Courthouse, but where a case is filed depends on where it happened and who's being sued. Confirm venue wording

How much does it cost?

Nothing up front. Injury cases are on a contingency fee: we're only paid if we recover money for you.
