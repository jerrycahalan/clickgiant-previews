After a serious injury

Taken to Springfield for treatment?

Springfield Memorial Hospital is designated by the State of Illinois as a Level I Trauma Center, so people badly hurt in crashes across central Illinois are often brought here, even when the wreck happened miles away.

If that's you or someone you love, focus on getting better. We'll gather the medical records and bills, talk to the insurance companies and keep you updated. If you can't come to us, we'll come to the hospital.

Know your rights

Rules that apply to every Springfield injury claim

2 years

To file an injury claim

Most injury claims must be filed within 2 years. Claims against a city, county or other local government have a shorter, 1-year deadline.

Source735 ILCS 5/13-202Illinois Code of Civil Procedure ↗
50% rule

Shared fault

You can still recover if you were 50% or less at fault. Your compensation is reduced by your share.

Source735 ILCS 5/2-1116Illinois Code of Civil Procedure ↗

Straight answers

Springfield injury FAQ

Why hire a Litchfield firm instead of a big Springfield firm?

At a big firm, your case may be handled mostly by staff you never meet. With us, Trent or Michael is your lawyer from day one. You'll have their attention, and when the case needs more firepower, we bring in experienced co-counsel and stay involved.

Do I have to drive to Litchfield?

No. We'll come to your home or hospital room in Springfield, or meet by phone or video. If you'd rather come in, our Litchfield office is the closest.

I was hurt in a crash on the interstate. Does that change anything?

Interstate crashes often involve trucks, out-of-town drivers or several vehicles, which means more insurance companies pointing fingers. We sort out who is responsible and deal with all of them for you. Trent/Michael to confirm

Where would my lawsuit be filed?

Most cases settle without a lawsuit. If one is needed, a Springfield case will often be filed at the Sangamon County Courthouse, but where a case is filed depends on where it happened and who's being sued. Confirm venue wording

How much does it cost?

Nothing up front. Injury cases are on a contingency fee: we're only paid if we recover money for you.
