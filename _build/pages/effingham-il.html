Effingham, Effingham County seat

Where two interstates meet

Effingham sits where I-57 and I-70 come together, which means a steady stream of semis, travelers and local traffic sharing the same roads. When a crash happens here, the other driver may be from another state and the insurance company may be hundreds of miles away. We handle that part so you can focus on getting better.

Effingham County is in the same judicial circuit as our home county. Both are part of Illinois' 4th Judicial Circuit, so we know how cases move through the courts here.

We'll meet you at home, in the hospital or by phone or video. Se habla español.

After a crash

Care close by, and a lawyer who comes to you

If you're hurt in or around Effingham, HSHS St. Anthony's Memorial Hospital on North Maple Street runs an emergency department staffed around the clock. Get checked out first. Some injuries don't show up for a day or two, and a record from the ER helps your claim later.

Then call us. You don't have to drive to our office. We'll come to your home or your hospital room, or meet by phone or video, and we'll start gathering the police report, photos and witness names while they're still fresh.

Most injury claims must be filed within 2 years. Claims against a city, county or other local government have a shorter, 1-year deadline.

Straight answers

Effingham injury FAQ

You're based in Montgomery County. Can you handle an Effingham County case?

Yes. Illinois lawyers can handle cases in any county in the state, and Effingham County is in the same judicial circuit as our home county. We'll appear at the courthouse in Effingham whenever your case needs it.

I was driving through on I-57 or I-70 and got hurt. I don't live in Illinois. Can you still help?

Yes. If the crash happened here, Illinois law usually applies, and we can handle your case while you're back home. Most of the work can be done by phone, email and video. Trent/Michael to confirm

A semi hit me. Is that different from a regular car crash?

Often, yes. Trucking companies carry larger insurance policies and have to follow federal safety rules, and they send their own investigators quickly. The sooner we're involved, the sooner we can ask them to preserve the truck's data and the driver's records.

Where would my lawsuit be filed?

Most cases settle without a lawsuit. If one is needed, an Effingham case will often be filed at the Effingham County Courthouse, but where a case is filed depends on where it happened and who's being sued. Confirm venue wording

How much does it cost?

Nothing up front. Injury cases are on a contingency fee: we're only paid if we recover money for you.
