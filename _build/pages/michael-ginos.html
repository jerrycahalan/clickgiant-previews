Michael G. Ginos

Michael is a third-generation Montgomery County lawyer. Before coming home to Hillsboro, he was part of a trial team taking on some of the largest pharmaceutical and medical device companies in the country.

Trial experience on both sides

In law school at Southern Illinois University, Michael worked as a licensed student attorney in the Civil Practice Clinic and clerked for an insurance defense firm. He saw early how insurance companies defend claims, which helps him anticipate their moves today.

He went on to serve as a prosecutor in the Montgomery County State's Attorney's Office, where he gained invaluable experience in the courtroom in front of many judges. He then spent two years at a large law firm handling complex cases against pharmaceutical and medical device manufacturers, along with personal injury cases. He worked on thousands of veterans' 3M Combat Arms Earplug cases and contributed to a trial team who helped win a $7.1 million federal jury verdict for three veterans.

Coming home

Michael's family has practiced law in Montgomery County since 1948. He joined Trent in June 2021 and is a partner in the Law Offices of West & Ginos, LLC. He handles personal injury cases personally, from the first call to the final check, and also helps clients with estate planning and criminal matters.

Hablamos español

Michael double majored in Communications, concentrating in organizations, and Spanish at the University of Illinois. He works directly with Spanish-speaking clients, with no interpreter needed. Se habla español.

Away from the office

Michael grew up in Hillsboro and graduated from Hillsboro High School. He is a member of the Hillsboro Lions Club, Rotary eClub of Central Illinois, and Montgomery County Bar Association. Michael has a passion for sports and helps coach local youth sports. He is an avid Illini fan, loves the outdoors, and loves helping people.
