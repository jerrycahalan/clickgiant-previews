Answers for you and your family

Mesothelioma is a cancer most often caused by breathing asbestos fibers. According to the National Cancer Institute, most mesotheliomas are thought to be due to asbestos exposure, and asbestos-related illness can take 10 to 40 years or more to show up. That means the exposure usually happened long ago: at a plant, a job site, a refinery, in the military, or even from a family member's work clothes.

These are complex cases. Finding out which products and companies were involved can mean tracing work history back decades. Where exposure happened

Jobs and places linked to asbestos

Construction & renovation

OSHA says heavy exposures tend to occur in construction, especially when old asbestos materials are removed during renovation, repair or demolition.

Pipe & boiler insulation

Asbestos was used to insulate steam lines, boilers and hot water pipes in plants, schools and power houses.

Building materials

Floor and ceiling tiles, paints, coatings and adhesives once contained asbestos.

Brakes & clutches

Mechanics who worked on vehicle brakes and clutches could be exposed.

Shipyards & the military

Ship repair and shipbuilding used asbestos to insulate boilers and pipes.

Family members

Loved ones can be exposed to fibers brought home on work clothes. Confirm with co-counsel

After a diagnosis

What to do after a mesothelioma diagnosis

Your health comes first. These steps also protect your family's claim.

  1. Focus on your treatment.Get care from doctors experienced with mesothelioma, and keep copies of your diagnosis and pathology reports.
  2. Write down your work history.List every job, employer, job site and years worked, plus any military service. Old coworkers can help fill in the gaps.
  3. Think about home and family exposure.Note any renovation projects, old homes or family members who worked around asbestos.
  4. Keep your records together.Medical bills, pay stubs, union cards, Social Security work history and old photos can all help trace exposure.
  5. Call us soon.Illinois deadlines are strict, and building a case takes time. We'll meet with you, or come to you, and explain your options for free.

Mesothelioma FAQ

What causes mesothelioma?

According to the National Cancer Institute, most mesotheliomas are thought to be due to asbestos exposure. The fibers can lodge in the lining of the lungs or abdomen and, over many years, lead to cancer.

I was exposed decades ago. Can I still have a case?

Often, yes. Asbestos illness can take 10 to 40 years or more to appear, so most cases involve exposure from long ago. Deadlines generally run from the diagnosis, not the exposure, but they're strict, so call soon. Confirm deadline wording

My employer is out of business. Who would be responsible?

Claims are often against the companies that made or supplied asbestos products, not just an employer. Part of our job is figuring out which products you were around and who made them.

Can my family file a claim if my loved one has passed away?

Yes, families may be able to bring a claim after a loved one dies from mesothelioma. We'll explain who can file and what's involved.

How much does it cost?

Nothing up front. We work on a contingency fee, so there's no fee unless your case recovers money.

Do I have to travel or go to court?

Usually very little. We can meet you at home or wherever is easiest, and we'll explain every step before it happens.
