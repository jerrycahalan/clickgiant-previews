After a crash

Care in town, and a lawyer who comes to you

Staunton has its own hospital. Community Hospital of Staunton on Caldwell Street is designated by the state as an Acute Stroke Ready Hospital. Whatever happened, get checked out first. Some injuries don't show up for a day or two, and a medical record helps your claim later.

Then call us. We'll come to you, and we'll start gathering the police report, photos and witness names while they're still fresh.

Know your rights

Rules that apply to every Staunton injury claim

2 years

To file an injury claim

Most injury claims must be filed within 2 years. Claims against a city, county or other local government have a shorter, 1-year deadline.

Shared fault

You can still recover if you were 50% or less at fault. Your compensation is reduced by your share.

Straight answers

Staunton injury FAQ

Macoupin County isn't your home county. Is that a problem?

No. Illinois lawyers can handle cases in any county in the state. Macoupin County is in the 7th Judicial Circuit, and we already handle cases there. If your case needs to be filed, we'll be at the courthouse in Carlinville with you.

Do I have to drive to Litchfield?

No. We'll come to your home or hospital room, or meet by phone or video. If you'd rather come in, our Litchfield office is the closest.

I was hurt on I-55 and I'm not from around here. Can you still help?

Yes. If the crash happened here, Illinois law usually applies, and we can handle your case while you're back home. Trent/Michael to confirm

Where would my lawsuit be filed?

Most cases settle without a lawsuit. If one is needed, a Staunton case will often be filed at the Macoupin County Courthouse in Carlinville, but where a case is filed depends on where it happened and who's being sued. Confirm venue wording

How much does it cost?

Nothing up front. Injury cases are on a contingency fee: we're only paid if we recover money for you.
