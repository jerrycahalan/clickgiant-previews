Litchfield sits where Interstate 55 meets Illinois Route 16, with historic Route 66 running through town on Columbian Boulevard. That means a lot of through traffic: semis on the interstate, travelers getting on and off at the exits, and local drivers heading east on Route 16 toward Hillsboro. We know these roads because we drive them every day.

Know your rights

Rules that apply to every Litchfield injury claim

2 years

To file an injury claim

Most injury claims must be filed within 2 years. Claims against a city, county or other local government have a shorter, 1-year deadline.

Source735 ILCS 5/13-202Illinois Code of Civil Procedure ↗
50% rule

Shared fault

You can still recover if you were 50% or less at fault. Your compensation is reduced by your share.

Where it's filed

Straight answers

Litchfield injury FAQ

Can I just stop by your Litchfield office?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome Monday through Friday, 8:30am to 5:00pm, at 413 N. State St. If you want to be sure a lawyer is free to sit down with you, call (217) 324-7777 first.

I was hurt on I-55 by a driver from out of state. Can you still help?

Yes. Interstate crashes often involve drivers and trucking companies from other states. We deal with their insurers wherever they're based. Trent/Michael to confirm

I'm in the hospital at St. Francis. Do I have to come to you?

No. We'll come to your hospital room or your home, or talk by phone or video. You can focus on getting better.

Will my case have to go to court in Hillsboro?

Most cases settle without a trial. If yours needs to be filed, a Litchfield case will usually be filed at the Montgomery County Courthouse in Hillsboro, but where a case is filed depends on where it happened and who's being sued. Confirm venue wording

How much does it cost?

Nothing up front. Injury cases are on a contingency fee: we're only paid if we recover money for you.
