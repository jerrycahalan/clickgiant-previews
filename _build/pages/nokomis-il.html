A Nokomis law office, run by a Nokomis kid

Trent West graduated from Nokomis High School. In 2016 he joined Michael Glenn's practice here in Nokomis, and that practice grew into the Law Offices of West & Ginos, LLC. Trent and his wife, Krystal, are raising their three children in the community, and he coaches local youth sports.

When someone from Nokomis gets hurt, they often know us already, from the ballfield or from Rotary. That matters. You're not a file number here. Trent or Michael handles your case personally, and you can reach us at the same office on West Central Street where it all started.

In a small town, the big risks are on the roads in and out: Route 16, county roads at harvest time, and the drive to work. We handle car and truck crashes, farm-equipment wrecks, dog bites, falls and work injuries for Nokomis families.

Know your rights

Rules that apply to every Nokomis injury claim

2 years

To file an injury claim

Most injury claims must be filed within 2 years. Claims against a city, county or other local government have a shorter, 1-year deadline.

Source735 ILCS 5/13-202Illinois Code of Civil Procedure ↗
50% rule

Shared fault

You can still recover if you were 50% or less at fault. Your compensation is reduced by your share.

Source735 ILCS 5/2-1116Illinois Code of Civil Procedure ↗
4th circuit

Where it's filed

Montgomery County cases are heard at the courthouse at 120 N. Main St. in Hillsboro, part of Illinois' 4th Judicial Circuit.

Straight answers

Nokomis injury FAQ

Is the Nokomis office a full office, or just a satellite?

It's a full office, and it's where the firm started. Call (217) 563-7777 or stop in Monday through Friday, 8:30am to 5:00pm. Either office can help with any case.

I know you from town. Is it awkward to hire you?

Not at all. Most of our clients know us from somewhere. Everything you tell us stays confidential.

I was hit by a tractor or farm truck. Who pays?

Farm vehicles are often covered by a farm or commercial policy, and the farm or business may also be responsible. We find every policy that applies. Trent/Michael to confirm

I can't get to the office. Can you come to me?

Yes. We'll meet you at home, in the hospital or by phone or video.

How much does it cost?

Nothing up front. Injury cases are on a contingency fee: we're only paid if we recover money for you.
