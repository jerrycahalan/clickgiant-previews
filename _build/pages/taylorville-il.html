Where your case is heard

A courthouse with Lincoln roots

Three courthouses have stood on the same square in Taylorville. Abraham Lincoln practiced law in the first one, finished in 1840. Today's Christian County Courthouse, with its clock tower and art glass dome, was dedicated in 1902.

A lot has changed since Lincoln's day, but one thing hasn't: insurance companies take a case seriously when they know the lawyer is ready for trial. We prepare every case that way.

Most injury claims must be filed within 2 years. Claims against a city, county or other local government have a shorter, 1-year deadline.

Straight answers

Taylorville injury FAQ

You're based in Montgomery County. Can you handle a Christian County case?

Yes. Illinois lawyers can handle cases in any county in the state, and Christian County is in the same judicial circuit as our home county. We'll appear at the courthouse in Taylorville whenever your case needs it.

Do I have to drive to your office?

No. We'll come to your home or hospital room in Taylorville, or meet by phone or video. If you'd rather come in, we have offices in Nokomis and Litchfield.

I was hurt in a crash with a farm vehicle. Who pays?

It depends on who owned and was driving the equipment and what insurance covers it. Farm policies can work differently from regular auto policies, so we review every policy that might apply. Trent/Michael to confirm

Where would my lawsuit be filed?

Most cases settle without a lawsuit. If one is needed, a Taylorville case will often be filed at the Christian County Courthouse, but where a case is filed depends on where it happened and who's being sued. Confirm venue wording

How much does it cost?

Nothing up front. Injury cases are on a contingency fee: we're only paid if we recover money for you.
