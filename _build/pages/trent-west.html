From prosecutor to injury lawyer

After law school, Trent started his career in 2011 at a private law firm in Vandalia, then served as a prosecutor in Madison County. Prosecutors learn how evidence holds up, how witnesses come across and what a judge or jury will believe. Trent uses those same lessons to prepare his clients' injury cases.

In 2016, Trent joined Michael Glenn in Nokomis. The practice became the Law Offices of Glenn & West, LLC in 2018 and, with Michael Ginos as his partner, the Law Offices of West & Ginos, LLC in 2026. Trent is the firm's Managing Partner.

How Trent works

When you hire the Law Offices of West & Ginos, LLC, Trent or Michael handles your case personally. Trent returns calls the same business day, explains each step in plain language and will meet you at home or in the hospital if it's hard to get to the office.

Personal injury is the center of his practice today. He also helps families with estate planning and probate and represents clients in criminal and traffic matters.

Away from the office

Trent graduated from Nokomis High School. He and his wife, Krystal, are raising their three children in the community, and Trent coaches local youth sports. He is Secretary and Past President of the Rotary eClub of Central Illinois, and he and Krystal's Stamp Out Cancer team helped raise money for the Montgomery County Cancer Association. Trent is an avid outdoorsman, enjoying deer hunting and bass fishing with his family and friends.
