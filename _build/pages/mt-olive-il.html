Local history

Home of the Union Miners Cemetery

Mt. Olive's Union Miners Cemetery was established by coal miners in 1899 and is the burial place of labor leader Mary Harris "Mother" Jones. Her 22-foot granite monument was unveiled in 1936, and the cemetery was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1972.

The miners who built it were fighting for safe work and fair pay. We think about that when someone comes to us hurt on the job or after a crash. You deserve someone in your corner who won't back down.

Straight answers

Mt. Olive injury FAQ

Macoupin County isn't your home county. Is that a problem?

No. Illinois lawyers can handle cases in any county in the state. Macoupin County is in the 7th Judicial Circuit, and we already handle cases there. If your case needs to be filed, we'll be at the courthouse in Carlinville with you.

Do I have to come to your office?

No. We'll come to your home or hospital room, or meet by phone or video. If you'd rather come in, our Litchfield office is the closest.

I was hurt at work. Do I need a lawyer for workers' comp?

You don't have to have one, but it helps. The insurance company has people working for it from day one. We can make sure your notice is given on time and that you get the benefits the law allows. See our workers' compensation page.

Where would my lawsuit be filed?

Most cases settle without a lawsuit. If one is needed, a Mt. Olive case will often be filed at the Macoupin County Courthouse in Carlinville, but where a case is filed depends on where it happened and who's being sued. Confirm venue wording

How much does it cost?

Nothing up front. Injury cases are on a contingency fee: we're only paid if we recover money for you.
