Straight answers

Before you call

Does the first consultation cost anything?

No. Injury consultations are free and there's no obligation to hire us.

Which office should I call?

Whichever is closer or easier. Either office can help with any case and get you to the right lawyer.

Do I need an appointment to stop by?

No. Walk-ins are welcome at either office. If you want to be sure a lawyer is free to sit down with you, give us a call before you come in.

I can't get to your office. Can we still meet?

Yes. We'll come to your home or hospital room, or meet by phone or video, anywhere in Illinois.

What should I have ready when I call?

Nothing is required. If you have them handy, a crash or incident report number, insurance letters and photos are helpful, but we can track those down for you.
