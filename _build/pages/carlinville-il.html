Carlinville, Macoupin County seat

Injury lawyers for Carlinville and Macoupin County

Carlinville is the Macoupin County seat and home to Blackburn College. It's a short drive west from our Litchfield office, and we regularly help people from Carlinville and the rest of Macoupin County after crashes, falls and other injuries.

Macoupin County is in a different judicial circuit from our home county. Montgomery County is in the 4th Circuit, while Macoupin County is in the 7th. If your case needs to be filed, it will usually be heard at the Macoupin County Courthouse on East Main Street, and we'll be there with you.

You don't have to come to us. We'll meet you at home, in your hospital room at Carlinville Area Hospital or by phone or video. Se habla español.

Where your case is heard

The "Million Dollar Courthouse"

The Macoupin County Courthouse was built from 1867 to 1870 at a cost of $1,342,226.31, a staggering sum at the time, which is how it earned its nickname. It's still where Macoupin County's court cases are heard today.

Different counties and circuits have their own judges and their own way of doing things. We handle cases outside our home county, and we prepare every case as if it's going to trial, because that's what gets insurance companies to take it seriously.

Rules that apply to every Carlinville injury claim

2 years

To file an injury claim

Most injury claims must be filed within 2 years. Claims against a city, county or other local government have a shorter, 1-year deadline.

Straight answers

Carlinville injury FAQ

You're based in Montgomery County. Can you handle a Macoupin County case?

Yes. Illinois lawyers can handle cases in any county in the state. We regularly help people from Macoupin County and will appear at the courthouse in Carlinville when your case needs it.

Do I have to drive to Litchfield?

No. We'll come to your home or hospital room in Carlinville, or meet by phone or video. If you'd rather come in, our Litchfield office is the closest.

I'm a Blackburn College student and got hurt. Can you help?

Yes. Students have the same rights as anyone else after a crash or fall. If your parents' insurance may apply, we'll sort that out too. Trent/Michael to confirm

Where would my lawsuit be filed?

Most cases settle without a lawsuit. If one is needed, a Carlinville case will often be filed at the Macoupin County Courthouse, but where a case is filed depends on where it happened and who's being sued. Confirm venue wording

How much does it cost?

Nothing up front. Injury cases are on a contingency fee: we're only paid if we recover money for you.
