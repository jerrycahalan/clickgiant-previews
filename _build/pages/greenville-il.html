Where your case is heard

Bond County's fourth courthouse

Bond County has had four courthouses. The first three, from 1823, 1833 and 1853, are gone. The one on West College Avenue was built in the 1880s and is still where Bond County's court cases are heard today.

When your case needs to be filed, we'll be there with you, prepared as if it's going to trial, because that's what gets insurance companies to take it seriously.

Straight answers

Greenville injury FAQ

You're based in Montgomery County. Can you handle a Bond County case?

Yes. Illinois lawyers can handle cases in any county in the state. Bond County is in the 3rd Judicial Circuit, the same circuit as Madison County, where Trent was a prosecutor.

Do I have to drive to your office?

No. We'll come to your home or hospital room, or meet by phone or video. If you'd rather come in, we have offices in Litchfield and Nokomis.

I'm a college student in Greenville. Can I hire you?

Yes. If you're an adult, you can hire us yourself, and your parents are welcome to be part of the conversation if you want them there. Trent/Michael to confirm

Where would my lawsuit be filed?

Most cases settle without a lawsuit. If one is needed, a Greenville case will often be filed at the Bond County Courthouse, but where a case is filed depends on where it happened and who's being sued. Confirm venue wording

How much does it cost?

Nothing up front. Injury cases are on a contingency fee: we're only paid if we recover money for you.
