Where your case is heard

Illinois' old capital

Vandalia was the capital of Illinois from 1820 to 1839. The Vandalia State House on West Gallatin Street, where a young Abraham Lincoln served in the Illinois House of Representatives, is now a state historic site.

Today, Fayette County's court cases are heard at the courthouse on South 7th Street. When your case needs to be filed, we'll be there with you, prepared as if it's going to trial, because that's what gets insurance companies to take it seriously.

Rules that apply to every Vandalia injury claim

2 years

To file an injury claim

Most injury claims must be filed within 2 years. Claims against a city, county or other local government have a shorter, 1-year deadline.

Straight answers

Vandalia injury FAQ

You're based in Montgomery County. Can you handle a Fayette County case?

Yes. Illinois lawyers can handle cases in any county in the state, and Fayette County is in the same judicial circuit as our home county. We'll appear at the courthouse in Vandalia whenever your case needs it.

Do I have to drive to your office?

No. We'll come to your home or hospital room, or meet by phone or video. If you'd rather come in, we have offices in Nokomis and Litchfield.

I was passing through on I-70 and got hurt. I don't live in Illinois. Can you still help?

Yes. If the crash happened here, Illinois law usually applies, and we can handle your case while you're back home. Most of the work can be done by phone, email and video. Trent/Michael to confirm

Where would my lawsuit be filed?

Most cases settle without a lawsuit. If one is needed, a Vandalia case will often be filed at the Fayette County Courthouse, but where a case is filed depends on where it happened and who's being sued. Confirm venue wording

How much does it cost?

Nothing up front. Injury cases are on a contingency fee: we're only paid if we recover money for you.
