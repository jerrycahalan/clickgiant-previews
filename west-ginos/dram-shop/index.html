When a bar keeps pouring, it can be responsible

Most people hurt by a drunk driver only think about the driver's insurance. But that policy is often small, and it can run out long before your medical bills do. Illinois has a law, called the Dram Shop Act, that lets injured people also bring a claim against the business that sold or served the alcohol.

The Law Offices of West & Ginos, LLC looks at every drunk driving and intoxication case for a dram shop claim. We find out where the person was drinking, get the receipts and video before they're gone, and make sure every source of money is on the table.

Don't wait on these cases. A dram shop claim has a much shorter deadline than most injury claims, and bar video is often erased within days.

Illinois dram shop law at a glance

Who can be responsible
The bar, restaurant or other business that sold or gave the alcohol, and in some cases the owner of the building
Do you have to prove fault?
You have to prove the business served the person who caused your injury, and that this caused or contributed to their intoxication. You don't have to prove the business was careless.
Deadline to file
1 year for the dram shop claim. Your claim against the driver usually has 2 years.
How much the bar can owe
Limited by law and adjusted every year. For 2026: $90,411.55 per person for injury or property damage, and $110,503.00 for loss of support or society
Cost to hire us
Nothing up front. No fee unless we win.

What to do after a crash with a drunk driver

If you can't do these yourself, ask a family member to help.

  1. Call 911 and get medical care.Tell the officer if you think the other driver was drinking, so it goes in the report.
  2. Write down what you heard.If the driver said where they had been, or you saw a receipt, cup or wristband, write it down while it's fresh.
  3. Get names of witnesses.People at the scene, or at the bar, may know how much the person drank.

Know your rights

How the Illinois Dram Shop Act works

1 year

Short deadline

A dram shop claim must be filed within one year after it arises. Courts treat this as a condition of the right to recover, so a late claim is lost.

SourceMorales v. Fail Safe (Ill. App. 1999)Illinois Courts ↗
No fault needed

The bar doesn't have to be careless

Illinois courts have said fault or wrongdoing by the business is not required for it to be liable. The claim is a special one created by the Act, with its own rules.

SourceMorales v. Fail Safe (Ill. App. 1999)Illinois Courts ↗
$90K+ 2026

Limits set each year

The Illinois Comptroller adjusts the limits every year for inflation. For judgments or settlements from January 20, 2026: $90,411.55 per person for injury or property damage, and $110,503.00 for loss of support or society.

Source235 ILCS 5/6-21(a)Illinois Comptroller, 2026 limits ↗
Who pays your bills

Where the money comes from after a drunk driving crash

A dram shop claim is one more source of money, on top of the others. We look for all of them.

  • The driver's insurance. Their liability coverage pays for your injuries, lost wages and pain and suffering.
  • The bar or restaurant. A dram shop claim against the business that served the alcohol, up to the limits set by law.
  • Your own auto policy. Uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage may apply when the driver has little or no insurance.
  • Your health insurance. It can cover treatment now, and we sort out repayment when the case settles.
Straight answers

Dram shop FAQ

What is a dram shop claim?

"Dram shop" is an old name for a bar. In Illinois, the Dram Shop Act lets a person injured by an intoxicated person bring a claim against the business that sold or served the alcohol, in addition to the claim against the person who hurt them.

The drunk driver has insurance. Why go after the bar too?

Many drivers carry only small policies, and serious injuries can cost far more. A dram shop claim adds another source of money for your medical bills, lost wages and pain and suffering.

How long do I have to file?

Only one year for the dram shop claim, which is half the time you usually have for an injury claim. Call as soon as you can so we can get the evidence and file on time.

How do you prove where the person was drinking?

Police reports, receipts and card statements, bar video, witnesses and social media posts can all show where someone was served and how much. Video is often erased quickly, so the sooner we start, the better.

What if they were drinking at a private party?

Private parties and social hosts usually aren't liable under the Dram Shop Act. It may be different if the party was at an event center, hotel or similar venue. Tell us what happened and we'll explain your options.

How much does it cost to hire you?

Nothing up front. We work on a contingency fee and are only paid if we recover money for you.
