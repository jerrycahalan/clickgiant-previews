The ethics rule

Illinois Rule 1.5(e) in plain terms

Illinois lets lawyers in different firms divide a fee only when three conditions are met. We follow all three on every referral.

1 basis

Work or joint responsibility

The fee is divided in proportion to the work each lawyer does, or, when your main role is the referral, each lawyer takes on joint financial responsibility for the representation, but either way we cover all expenses.

SourceIll. R. Prof'l Conduct 1.5(e)(1)Illinois Courts ↗
2 in writing

The client agrees

Your client agrees to the arrangement, including the share each lawyer will receive, and that agreement is confirmed in writing.

SourceIll. R. Prof'l Conduct 1.5(e)(2)Illinois Courts ↗
3 total fee

The total fee is reasonable

Dividing the fee never raises what your client pays. The total fee has to be reasonable, the same as if one firm handled the case alone.

SourceIll. R. Prof'l Conduct 1.5(e)(3)Illinois Courts ↗

Questions from attorneys

Referral FAQ

What kinds of cases do you take?

Personal injury cases of all sizes: car, truck and motorcycle crashes, dog bites, falls, wrongful death and catastrophic injuries. If you're not sure, call us. We'll give you a straight answer.

Will my client know about the referral fee?

Yes. Illinois requires your client to agree to how the fee is divided, including each lawyer's share, and that agreement must be confirmed in writing. The total fee your client pays doesn't go up because of the referral.

Do I have to work on the case?

That's up to you. Under Rule 1.5(e), the fee can be divided in proportion to the work each lawyer does, or, if your main role is the referral, by each lawyer taking on joint financial responsibility for the representation. We'll talk through which fits before anything is signed, but most attorneys that refer us cases let us handle the matter and they just wait for their check.

How will I hear about the case?

We update you at every major step, and you can call Trent or Michael directly whenever you want to check in.

What if you can't take the case?

We'll tell you quickly, explain why, and point your client in the right direction if we can.
