Step by step

What to do if you get a ticket or are charged

What you do in the first few days can make a real difference.

  1. Stay calm and be polite.Give the officer your license and insurance. Don't argue at the scene, even if you think the stop was unfair.
  2. Don't explain or discuss the case.You have the right to remain silent. Anything you say can be used in court, so save the explaining for your lawyer.
  3. Write down what happened.Times, places, what was said, and who was there, while it's fresh.
  4. Keep every piece of paper.Your ticket, bond slip and any notice of suspension list court dates and deadlines you can't miss.
  5. Don't just pay the ticket.Paying a ticket can count as pleading guilty and put a conviction on your driving record. Ask about your options first. Trent to confirm
  6. Watch the DUI hearing deadline.After a DUI arrest, you have 90 days from the suspension notice to ask a judge for a hearing on it.
  7. Call us before your court date.The sooner we see the paperwork, the more options you'll have.

Know your rights

Illinois DUI license rules

46 th day

When the suspension starts

After a DUI arrest, a statutory summary suspension of your license takes effect on the 46th day after you're given notice. It's separate from the criminal case.

Source625 ILCS 5/11-501.1Illinois Vehicle Code ↗
90 days

To ask for a hearing

You have 90 days after the suspension notice to file a written request for a hearing in circuit court to challenge the suspension.

Source625 ILCS 5/2-118.1Illinois Vehicle Code ↗
6–12 months

First-offender suspension

For a first offender, the suspension is 6 months after a test of 0.08 or more, and 12 months after refusing testing.

Source625 ILCS 5/6-208.1Illinois Vehicle Code ↗

Straight answers

Traffic and criminal defense FAQ

Should I just pay my traffic ticket?

Not before you understand what it means for your record. Paying a ticket can count as a guilty plea, and convictions can add up toward a license suspension. Call us first and we'll go over your options. Trent to confirm

Will I lose my license after a DUI arrest?

Probably, unless the suspension is successfully challenged. Illinois suspends your license separately from the criminal case. It starts on the 46th day after you get notice. For a first offender, it lasts 6 months after a test of 0.08 or more and 12 months after a refusal. You have 90 days to ask for a hearing, so call us right away.

Do I really need a lawyer for a misdemeanor?

A misdemeanor conviction can stay on your record and affect jobs, housing and professional licenses. A lawyer can often find options you wouldn't know to ask for.

Do you only handle cases in Montgomery County?

No. We handle cases in Montgomery, Macoupin and surrounding counties. Call us and we'll tell you if we can help with yours.
