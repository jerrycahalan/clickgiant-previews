Why the Law Offices of West & Ginos, LLC

Six reasons injured people choose us

01

We return calls the same business day

The most common complaint about lawyers is that they don't call back. Reach out during the week and you'll hear from us that same business day, whether it's a new case or a question about yours.

02

Your lawyer handles your case

Trent or Michael handles your case personally, from the first call to the final check. Our support team answers your calls and keeps your paperwork moving, so you're never left wondering where things stand.

03

We come to you

Hospital room, kitchen table or job site. If an injury makes it hard to get to our office, we'll come to you anywhere in Illinois.

04

Big-firm experience, local roots

Both partners are former prosecutors. Michael spent two years on a complex-litigation trial team before coming home. We know Montgomery County's roads, hospitals and courthouses, and we'll travel anywhere in Illinois for your case.

05

No fee on an offer you already had

If an insurer makes an offer before you hire us, we don't take a fee from that money. We earn our fee on what we add.

06

No fee unless we win

Your consultation is free, and you pay nothing up front on an injury case. We work on contingency and are only paid when we recover money for you.

Straight answers

Questions about working with us

Will I actually talk to a lawyer?

Yes. Trent or Michael handles your case personally. Our support team helps with scheduling and paperwork, but your lawyer is the one making decisions about your case and talking with you about them.

How quickly will you call me back?

The same business day. If you call or fill out our form during the week, you'll hear from us that day.

Do you only handle cases in Montgomery County?

No. Our offices are in Litchfield and Nokomis, and we regularly help clients in Springfield, Carlinville, Edwardsville, Effingham and other towns across Central Illinois. We'll travel anywhere in Illinois when a case needs it.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. Michael speaks Spanish and works directly with Spanish-speaking clients. Se habla español.

How much does it cost to hire you?

Nothing up front. We work on a contingency fee and are only paid if we recover money for you. If an insurer makes an offer before you hire us, we don't take a fee from that money.
