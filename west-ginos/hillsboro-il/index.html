Hillsboro's hometown injury lawyer

Michael Ginos graduated from Hillsboro High School and came home in 2021 after working on a trial team that took on some of the largest pharmaceutical and medical device companies in the country. He's a third-generation Montgomery County lawyer, a member of the Hillsboro Lions Club and a former Montgomery County prosecutor.

Hillsboro is the Montgomery County seat, so it's where most injury cases from around the county end up if they're filed in court. Michael has spent years in that courthouse, first as a prosecutor and now for injured people.

Where your case is heard

We know the Montgomery County Courthouse

The historic Montgomery County Courthouse on Courthouse Square was first built in 1833–35 and remodeled in 1868–71. Today, court cases are handled at 120 N. Main St., with the circuit clerk's office in the Courts Complex just north of the historic building.

Michael worked in the Montgomery County State's Attorney's Office, where he gained experience in front of many judges. Trent is a past president of the Montgomery County Bar Association. When your case needs to be filed, you'll have lawyers who know this courthouse well.

Know your rights

Rules that apply to every Hillsboro injury claim

2 years

To file an injury claim

Most injury claims must be filed within 2 years. Claims against a city, county or other local government have a shorter, 1-year deadline.

Source735 ILCS 5/13-202Illinois Code of Civil Procedure ↗
50% rule

Shared fault

You can still recover if you were 50% or less at fault. Your compensation is reduced by your share.

Source735 ILCS 5/2-1116Illinois Code of Civil Procedure ↗
Straight answers

Hillsboro injury FAQ

Do you have an office in Hillsboro?

Our offices are in Litchfield, about 9 miles west on Route 16, and in Nokomis. We're in Hillsboro all the time for court, and we're happy to meet you at your home or in the hospital if that's easier. Confirm meeting options in Hillsboro

Michael used to be a prosecutor here. Does that matter for my injury case?

It means he knows how evidence holds up in this courthouse and what a Montgomery County jury will want to see. He uses that to build your case.

I was hurt at Lake Glenn Shoals. Can you help?

Yes. We handle boating, jet ski and dock injuries. Boating cases can have different rules than car crashes, so talk to us early. See our boating accident page.

Can I talk to a lawyer in Spanish?

Yes. Michael speaks Spanish and works directly with Spanish-speaking clients, with no interpreter needed. Se habla español.

How much does it cost?

Nothing up front. Injury cases are on a contingency fee: we're only paid if we recover money for you.
